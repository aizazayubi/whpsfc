Where the club comes from

WPECHS FC carries the name of the WAPDA & PESCO Employees Cooperative Housing Society in Kohat — a community built around service, and now a football club built around the same idea. The badge on our chest is the neighbourhood we play for.

What sets this squad apart isn't just results. It's the dressing room. Players who grew up two streets apart, coaches who know every family by name, and a bench that backs each other up like brothers before it backs up the scoreline.
